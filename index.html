FCR Delivery Partner Workshop

Financial Consolidation and Reporting — self-paced delivery training for Anaplan implementation partners

About This Workshop

This lab guide is your reference companion for the FCR Delivery Partner Workshop. It covers everything you need to implement Anaplan's Financial Consolidation and Reporting (FCR) product from scratch — from tenant provisioning through period management, consolidation rules, and security configuration.

FCR is one of the most complex products in the Anaplan portfolio. This guide is designed so you can return to any section after the workshop and use it as a reference during your first live implementation.

ℹ Training Tenants

Your FCR training tenant credentials were provided when you enrolled. Tenants remain active for approximately 5 weeks after you start. PC users have full access including Excel-based input forms. Mac users require a Windows VM for Excel add-in features.

Who This Is For

✅ Intended Audience

  • Partner delivery consultants implementing FCR for customers
  • Consultants with consolidation accounting background (HFM, OneStream, Cognos Controller, or similar)
  • Anaplan workspace experience preferred but not required

🎯 What You'll Be Able to Do

  • Provision and configure an FCR tenant from scratch
  • Build the complete data model: dimensions, accounts, ownership
  • Configure currency conversion, journals, and consolidation rules
  • Initialize new periods and manage month-end close
  • Deliver FCR independently (with Anaplan Professional Services support on the first engagement)
