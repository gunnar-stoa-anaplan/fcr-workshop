Welcome

You're about to learn one of the most complex products in the Anaplan portfolio. FCR — Financial Consolidation and Reporting — is not a simple configuration exercise. It's a full statutory consolidation engine: multi-entity, multi-currency, intercompany elimination, journals, period management, and regulatory reporting. Getting it right requires understanding both the product and the underlying accounting concepts it represents.

You're Not Alone

Most people taking this workshop are implementing FCR for the first or second time. The learning curve is steep — steeper than most Anaplan products. FCR originated from Fluence Technologies (acquired by Anaplan in recent years), and it has its own vocabulary, its own logic, and its own failure modes that are different from what you'd expect coming from HFM, Cognos Controller, or OneStream.

That's exactly why this workshop exists. This self-paced, hands-on, live-tenant program compresses what would otherwise take months of trial and error into a structured learning path you work through on your own schedule.

Why This Matters

FCR closes the loop on the Office of CFO story. Customers who already own IFP for planning need FCR for actuals — group consolidation, currency translation, intercompany elimination, and statutory financial statements. Partners who can deliver both IFP and FCR become indispensable to their clients and to Anaplan's go-to-market motion.

The combined FPC (Financial Planning and Consolidation) SKU is Anaplan's primary Office of CFO offering. If you can only deliver IFP, you're half the story. Mastering FCR makes you the complete story.

What to Expect

Self-paced, hands-on, live tenants, real exercises. By the end you'll be able to implement FCR independently — with Anaplan Professional Services support on your first project. That last part is not a caveat; it's intentional. FCR is complex enough that even experienced consultants benefit from Professional Services oversight on their first live engagement. This workshop gets you to the point where that support is a quality gate, not a learning crutch.

Who It's For

👤 Audience

How to Use This Guide

This guide is organized by workflow, not by UI screen. Each section covers a self-contained topic and follows Tell → Show → Do in that order:

📖 Tell Concepts What the feature is, how it works, what the limits are. Factual and reference-focused.
👁 Show Demo Moments Screenshots and step-by-step navigation of what you'll see in the product during the upcoming lab.
✅ Do Hands-On Labs Numbered step-by-step instructions in your training tenant. Complete each lab in the tenant provided when you enrolled.

Lab sections (pages 06, 08, 10, 14, 17) contain Mini-Lab blocks with numbered steps, time estimates, and completion checklists. Complete them in order — each lab builds on the previous one.

⚠ Training Tenant Access

Use your assigned training tenant only. Do not work in a customer tenant during workshop exercises. Credentials were sent when you enrolled — check the shared SharePoint folder if you don't have yours. Tenant access expires approximately 5 weeks after you start. Mac users require a Windows VM for Excel-based input forms.
